# Instructor notes

Grade the quality of the comparison, not a single magical parameter value. Every individual student should use the same map, initial pose, scenario identifiers, and goal poses so trial results remain comparable.

Do not require students to implement a planner. The learning target is interpreting planner output, designing controlled navigation trials, and turning a human-centered concern into testable configuration and behavior.

During Mission 3, require the baseline to succeed geometrically. If it collides or cannot reach the goal, it does not isolate the intended distinction between collision avoidance and social appropriateness. A redesign that merely stops forever also fails: it must reach the goal while meeting the course clearance and speed requirements.

Keep discussion of personal space contextual. Invite students to identify accessibility, cultural, urgency, and environmental assumptions and to propose human confirmation or policy selection where a fixed radius is insufficient.
