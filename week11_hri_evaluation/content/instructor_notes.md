# Instructor notes

Pair students only for the participant role. Each student should facilitate their own five baseline and five redesign trials, maintain their own random participant code, implement their own changes, and submit their own interpretation.

This exercise is formative and should not collect sensitive or identifiable information. Stop the activity if the local institution requires a review or procedure not covered by the course protocol.

Keep motion disabled for ordinary peer tests. If physical motion is demonstrated, use instructor supervision, a clear workspace, low speed, a hardware emergency stop, and the institution's robot safety procedures; the software stop in this repository is not a certified safety device.

Grade traceability: a strong redesign connects a specific baseline observation to a concrete interface/state-machine change and then tests the predicted consequence. Do not reward unsupported claims of universal accessibility or usability from one participant.
