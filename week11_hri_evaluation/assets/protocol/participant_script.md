# Participant script

Thank you for helping test a course prototype. You are testing the system, not being tested yourself. Participation is voluntary; you may skip any task or stop at any time without giving a reason.

The evaluator will record only a random participant code, task outcomes, ratings, timing, and non-identifying notes. Do not provide your name, demographic information, disability or medical information. No audio, video, photo, or screen recording is permitted for this lab.

After each scenario, answer the five short questions in the evaluator's form. Use the stop control at any time if the robot behavior is uncomfortable or unclear.
